# Post-launch stabilization

Stage 16 starts only after a controlled pilot exits Stage 15. For the next two
releases, prioritize correctness and recovery over feature expansion.

Weekly review: availability/error/latency, authentication anomalies, outbox/scan
lag, open attendance exceptions, leave-ledger reconciliation, payroll variance,
support volume and recurring root causes. Compare every pilot payroll period against
the approved source calculation and classify each variance by input, policy,
rounding, software, or manual-source error.

Do not graduate from stabilization while a high-severity correctness issue recurs,
recovery evidence is stale, or variance is unexplained. Future notifications,
analytics, reports, payslip PDFs, custom Time-Off Officer role, bulk import/export,
mobile/PWA, biometrics and statutory automation each need a problem statement,
data/permission impact, acceptance criteria, operational owner and rollback plan
before implementation.
